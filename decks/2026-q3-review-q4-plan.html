    01 / Q3 highlights · what it moved

    What moved meeting-reported

    01 · Brazil funnel

    Inbound rebuilt as a pipeline, not a contact list

    Problem
    Stage-1 deals fell from ~150 (May) to ~50 (Aug). MQLs buried under bad ones, no SLA, one overwhelmed SDR.
    Shipped
    Lead-object pipeline (Sep 1), 11-step cadence with Lucas, 10-day automated sequence (Sep 25), LatAm inbound dashboard Lucas uses daily, Mexico in the same view.
    Still missing

    Exact denominator and whether "contact" means an attempt or a two-way conversation. Do not call 9/10 a 90% connection rate. MQL→S1 sat at ~10% in September because all ~307 leads were classified MQL.

    02 · Seller tools

    Sellers now pick from Spotter, with Wake the Dead inside it

    Moved
    ~50 logos claimed by ADRs in 15 minutes at launch (Aug 13). 72 logos worked in one week, ~250 in stage 1 (Sep 1). Spotter live with self-serve uploads; Mexico and EMEA lists in Sniper (Sep 22–25).
    Still missing

    A batch-level funnel from suggested logo to accepted, worked, S1, S2 and won. Selection is not conversion. Evan, Sep 1: "We still need to see the revenue impact."

    03 · TMD and expansion

    A working activation motion for the Oct 15 event

    Still missing

    Downstream pipeline from attendees. UG2 closed its pilot at 0 of 10 expansion opportunities (see scorecard). Customer share of the list is reported as 7% in one note and 70% in another; reconcile before using either.

    01 / Q3 highlights · lessons
    The reporting loop is not closed

    Wake the Dead, UG2, inbound before/after: a live dashboard is progress, not a reconciled readout with cohort and attribution rules.

    Q4 fixR03 funnel autopsy, R05 batch-level funnel, one report linked in the 1:1 doc.
    Shared data problems hit everything at once

    Plant identity, regional associations, duplicate plants and logo-level locking show up in expansion, Spotter, international lists and page generation.

    Q4 fixR07 plant object (David, Ramalho, Malu) and R06 Sniper→HubSpot handoff, solved once.
    Launches confused with adoption

    A demo gets enthusiasm; the page builder had one real link in the Sep 21 review. Accepting a logo, generating a page and booking a meeting are different events.

    Q4 fixSeller activation defined as "accepted AND worked"; R08 usage tracking.
    Process changes need a joint owner

    Attribution changes, autoresponder overlap, disqualification rules and UG2 complaints all crossed Growth, Pre-sales and Lifecycle without one change record.

    Q4 fixA short owner-and-handoff agreement, plus the attribution dispute doc (Oct 1 action).
    Capacity distorted the roadmap

    Performance backfill open since July, SDR turnover removed tribal knowledge, and an all-Brazil focus let the US decline go unnoticed.

    Q4 fixWIP limit of three programs; interim paid coverage with an owner, a limit and an exit (R19).
    Source: Q3 Growth Review, "The gaps that keep recurring" (Sep 28) · Evan 1:1 Aug 26, Sep 1, Sep 25.
